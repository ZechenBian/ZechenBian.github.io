Glimmers on curves of higher genus
T. Bell
2033

Abstract. We extend Ash's splitting theorem for glimmers to curves of genus g, at the
cost of g twists (Theorem 2.1).

1. Set-up
We use the glimmers of [A2, Definition 1.1].
1.1 Lemma. A glimmer on a curve of genus g restricts to a glimmer on every open subset.
Proof. Restriction preserves the zero count. ∎
<<<page 2>>>
2. The main result
2.1 Theorem. Every glimmer (F, s) on a curve of genus g splits after g + 1 twists.
Proof. On each rational piece apply [A2, Theorem 1.3]; glue with Lemma 1.1. Compare
also [A2, Example 4.2] for the case g = 0. ∎
2.2 Remark. For g = 0 this recovers [A2, Theorem 1.3].
