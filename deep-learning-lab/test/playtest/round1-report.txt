PLAYTEST REPORT (desktop 1360x900, phone 390x844)

A. What it wants, and what I concluded

A study companion to a deep-learning course for mathematicians. Per week: play with a figure, read labelled statements with folded proofs, ask a tutor about any line, then predict the outcome of a training run, run it, and learn whether the prediction held and why. The first screen says so plainly; "Begin with week 1" is obvious.

- Week 1 figure (three hinges): a sum of ReLU hinges fits a curve; gradient descent does the fitting and can stall. Bends 2, -2, 2 made the loss worse (0.673); 2, -4, 2 at kinks -0.5, 0, 0.5 gave 0. "What you just did" confirmed those numbers, and its suggested bad start stalled as promised (0.166).
- Week 1 experiments: backprop exactness, predicted "below 1e-6", held (1.86e-8). Zero init, predicted chance, held (33.0%); on the phone I chose "worse, but above chance" and was corrected. Step size x10, predicted failure, held, for a reason new to me ("Nothing overflowed": dead ReLUs). PyTorch (cannot run here): the reference run corrected me, GPU 7.5 times slower at width 32.
- Week 4 figure (narrow valley): blow-up just above eta x kappa = 2 at kappa 25 and 100; momentum cut 76 steps to 18. Confirmed, but see D1.
- Week 4 experiments: optimizers, predicted about 300 steps, held (286); adversarial, predicted 60-95%, corrected (42.9% at d = 1024).
- Week 7 figure (soft lookup): output 4.00 with the query along D; sharpness 0 gives the plain average 3.00; query length acts exactly like sharpness. Confirmed.
- Week 7 experiments: without 1/sqrt(d_k), predicted over 60%, held; transformer (cannot run), reference run, held.
